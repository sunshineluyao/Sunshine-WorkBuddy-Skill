## Long description / 详细介绍

### 简体中文

面向科研人员、教育者、创作者、顾问和高技能自由职业者，结合用户的专业、兴趣、作品、网络与地点约束，通过中英双语实时检索和证据分级，生成：

1. 一句话跨界定位；
2. 现金引擎、证据资产与长期选择权；
3. 带继续、调整和停止条件的90天灯塔实验；
4. 风险、冲突与待核验事项；
5. 经用户授权、根据真实结果更新建议的显式学习账本。

基础画像无需外部账号；实时机会扫描依赖宿主提供的可用网页检索能力。Skill 不承诺收入，也不替代法律、税务、移民、医疗或投资专业意见。

### English

Designed for researchers, educators, creators, consultants, and skilled independent professionals, this bilingual skill combines expertise, interests, artifacts, networks, and location constraints with current Chinese- and English-language research. It produces:

1. a one-sentence interdisciplinary position;
2. a near-term cash engine, reusable evidence asset, and long-term option;
3. a 90-day lighthouse experiment with continue, adjust, and stop gates;
4. risks, source conflicts, and unresolved verification items; and
5. an explicit, user-approved learning ledger that updates later recommendations from observed outcomes.

The base profile requires no external account. A current opportunity scan depends on web research capabilities available in the host. The skill does not promise income and is not a substitute for legal, tax, immigration, medical, or investment advice.
